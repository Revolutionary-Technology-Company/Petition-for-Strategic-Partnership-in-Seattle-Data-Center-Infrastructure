# Petition for Strategic Partnership in Seattle Data Center Infrastructure

## Overview
This repository contains the official petition and supporting documentation proposing a strategic partnership to construct and manage a new class of high-performance data centers in Seattle, Washington. 

Addressed to Seattle Mayor Katie Wilson, this proposal outlines a self-sustaining, zero-debt financial model to build next-generation data infrastructure. By leveraging federal research funding from the Pacific Northwest National Laboratories (PNNL) and cutting-edge NVIDIA Quantum-X800 networking technology, this project aims to overcome the power constraint and municipal debt issues that prompted the City Council's proposed data center moratorium.

## Key Stakeholders
* **City of Seattle:** Public Resources and Land Use.
* **State of Washington:** State Resource Administration
* **Revolutionary Technology:** Lead architects of the Quantum Circuit framework.
* **NVIDIA:** Provider of the Quantum-X800 InfiniBand networking platform (800 Gb/s connectivity).
* **Pacific Northwest National Laboratories (PNNL):** Federal funding source and primary initial tenant for AI research workloads.
* **Andro Hydro:** Clean energy partner facilitating off-grid hydroelectric power transfer.
* **University of Washington:** Academic, engineering, and labor sourcing partner.
* **Seattle Pacific University:** Academic, engineering, and labor sourcing partner
* **Fox Rothschild:** Legal framework and government contract management.

## Repository Contents

* **`Data-Center-Funding-Katie-Wilson.pdf` / `.docx`**
    The formal petition letter addressed to Mayor Katie Wilson. It details the strategic alignment between Revolutionary Technology, NVIDIA CEO Jensen Huang, and the U.S. Government to fund and build this infrastructure without taxing municipal resources.

* **`Addendum_A.md`**
    The *365-Day Fiscal Viability & Profitability Model*. This document uses mathematical modeling to plot the exact timeline of profitability, proving that the PNNL contract yields net-positive capital to fund independent commercial data centers *before* the proposed 365-day municipal moratorium expires.

* **`hydroelectric_opportunities_Washington.md`**
    A research brief detailing abandoned, non-operational, or "deadbeat" hydroelectric opportunities in Washington State (such as Enloe Dam and Electron Dam). This supports the initiative's goal of retrofitting existing infrastructure to provide dedicated "behind-the-meter" power, bypassing the Seattle City Light power grid.
