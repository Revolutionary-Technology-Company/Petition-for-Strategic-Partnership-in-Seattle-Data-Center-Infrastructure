1. **Grid Instability:** Facilities were drawing tens of megawatts from municipal grids, threatening localized brownouts and driving up utility rates.  
2. **Thermal Roadblocks:** The immense heat generated by legacy servers required millions of gallons of water for evaporative cooling, an untenable solution in water-scarce regions.
